How we work

Our Process

Four steps, no mystery. You will know what happens next at every stage of your remodel.

01

Design consultation

We measure the room, talk through how you cook or get ready in the morning, and sketch the layouts worth pricing.

02

Selections & estimate

You choose cabinetry, stone, tile and fixtures against real allowances. The estimate is itemised and reviewed line by line.

03

Demo & build

Zip walls and floor protection go up first. Then demo, rough-in, inspections, cabinetry, stone, tile and trim — in that order.

04

Walkthrough & warranty

A punch-list walkthrough, a swept site, and a written workmanship warranty on everything we installed.
